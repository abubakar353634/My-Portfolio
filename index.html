The Story

From a quiet four months to a store called PixelPortech

I started learning Shopify development in February 2025 — store structure, product research, competitor breakdowns, the works. The first few months were quiet. Four, five months without a single order, just building skills in the dark and trusting the process.

Then it clicked. A client on Fiverr took a chance on me, and that project became PixelPortech — a structured, trust-first store now live and serving customers across the US, built with clean product pages, real social proof, and zero clutter.

That's the standard I hold every project to now. I'm not a "theme installer." I dig into what makes a product sell, study what competitors are getting wrong, and use AI-assisted coding to build custom Liquid, CSS, and HTML functionality that off-the-shelf themes simply can't do.

Based in Lahore, working with clients across Pakistan, the Gulf, and beyond — building stores that look premium and perform even better.

What I Do

01

02

Winning Product Research
